How we work

A disciplined path from finding to fixed.

Whether the goal is certification, a hardened cloud, or a tested SOC, our engagements follow the same arc so you always know where you stand.

01

Assess

Interviews, configuration review, and technical testing establish an evidence-based current state, measured against the framework or threat model that matters to you.

02

Design

A prioritized roadmap and target architecture that balance risk reduction, effort, and business constraints, with clear owners and milestones.

03

Implement

We work alongside your teams to build it: policies, controls, infrastructure as code, detections, and identity integrations, with knowledge transfer throughout.

04

Validate & Sustain

Controls are tested, evidence is packaged for auditors, and operating rhythms (metrics, reviews, retests) keep the posture from drifting.
