Offensive & Defensive Security

We emulate the adversaries you actually face, then help your defenders detect, contain, and recover faster, closing the loop between attack and defense.

Cyber Defense

Build and mature the people, processes, and technology that detect and stop attacks: from logging architecture and SIEM engineering to threat hunting and incident response readiness.

Most organizations own capable security tools but lack the telemetry, tuning, and playbooks to make them effective. We assess your security operations against the MITRE ATT&CK framework and your real threat landscape, close visibility gaps, engineer high-fidelity detections, and prepare your team to respond decisively when an incident occurs. Engagements range from a focused SOC maturity assessment to a multi-month build-out of detection and response capability.

What's included

  • SOC maturity assessment covering people, process, technology, and metrics
  • Log source strategy and centralized logging architecture (identity, endpoint, network, cloud, SaaS)
  • SIEM and SOAR engineering on Microsoft Sentinel, Splunk, Google Security Operations, or Elastic
  • Detection engineering: use-case design, detection-as-code, tuning to reduce false positives
  • MITRE ATT&CK coverage mapping against threat-intelligence-prioritized techniques
  • Hypothesis-driven threat hunting across endpoint, identity, and cloud telemetry
  • Incident response plan, role-specific playbooks (ransomware, BEC, cloud compromise, insider)
  • Executive and technical tabletop exercises
  • Vulnerability management program design with risk-based prioritization

Deliverables

  • ATT&CK detection coverage heatmap (current vs. target)
  • Logging standard with required sources, fields, and retention
  • Version-controlled detection rule library with documentation
  • Incident response plan and playbooks, aligned to NIST SP 800-61
  • Threat hunt reports with findings and new detections
  • Tabletop after-action report with improvement actions
Outcome: Measurably shorter time to detect and respond, fewer noisy alerts, and a team that has rehearsed its response before it matters.

Red Team

Objective-based adversary emulation that tests your organization's ability to prevent, detect, and respond to a determined, stealthy attacker, end to end.

Unlike a penetration test, which aims to find as many vulnerabilities as possible, a red team engagement pursues specific business objectives (access to crown-jewel data, domain dominance, fraudulent payment initiation) the way a real threat actor would. We build scenarios from threat intelligence relevant to your industry, operate covertly against your live defenses, and document precisely where controls held and where they failed. Every engagement runs under formal rules of engagement with a trusted-agent deconfliction process to keep operations safe.

What's included

  • Threat-intelligence-led scenario design and agreed objectives ("flags")
  • Rules of engagement, legal authorization, and white-cell deconfliction
  • External reconnaissance and attack surface mapping
  • Initial access via phishing, vishing, exposed services, or credential attacks
  • Assumed-breach scenarios starting from a standard user workstation
  • Active Directory and Entra ID attack paths, privilege escalation, lateral movement
  • Cloud control-plane attacks across Azure, AWS, and GCP
  • EDR and SOC evasion testing using custom tooling and tradecraft
  • Optional physical intrusion and social engineering components

Deliverables

  • Executive summary of business impact and objectives achieved
  • Full attack narrative and timeline mapped to MITRE ATT&CK
  • Detection-and-response gap analysis (what was seen, missed, or ignored)
  • Technical findings with risk ratings and remediation guidance
  • Indicators of compromise and artifacts for your blue team
  • Executive and technical debrief sessions
  • Optional replay as a purple team exercise
Outcome: An honest, evidence-based answer to "could a real attacker achieve this, and would we notice?", plus a prioritized plan to make the answer "no".
Adversary emulationAssumed breachActive DirectoryCloud attack pathsSocial engineering

Purple Team

Collaborative, transparent exercises where our offensive operators and your defenders work side by side to test, measure, and improve detection technique by technique.

Purple teaming converts offensive insight directly into defensive improvement. Together we select ATT&CK techniques used by the threat groups most relevant to you, execute them in your environment, and observe in real time whether your controls prevent, alert, or stay silent. Where gaps appear, we write and tune detections in the session and re-run the technique to prove they work. The result is a validated, continuously improvable detection baseline rather than a point-in-time report.

What's included

  • Threat profile selection and technique test plan mapped to ATT&CK
  • Controlled execution of techniques using Atomic Red Team, Caldera, and custom tooling
  • Real-time observation of EDR, SIEM, and network control responses
  • Joint triage: prevented, detected, logged-only, or missed
  • In-session detection engineering and tuning
  • Retest to validate new and improved detections
  • Analyst coaching on investigation and response workflows
  • Setup for continuous, automated breach-and-attack simulation

Deliverables

  • Technique-level scorecard (prevent / detect / respond)
  • Before-and-after ATT&CK coverage heatmaps
  • New and tuned detection rules, ready to deploy
  • Logging and telemetry gaps with remediation steps
  • Repeatable test cases for regression testing
  • Prioritized backlog for remaining gaps
Outcome: Proven detection coverage for the techniques that matter most, and a blue team that understands attacker behavior first-hand.

Web Application Testing

In-depth security assessment of your web applications and APIs, including authenticated, role-based, and business-logic testing, plus integration of security into the software development lifecycle.

Web application testing focuses on the application itself: how it handles authentication, sessions, authorization, input, data, and business workflows. Using the OWASP Application Security Verification Standard (ASVS) as a baseline, we test every user role, every API, and every workflow for flaws that automated scanners cannot find, such as broken access control, logic abuse, and multi-step attack chains. For teams that ship continuously, we also help embed security into development with threat modeling, secure code review, and pipeline tooling.

What's included

  • OWASP Top 10 and ASVS-aligned verification
  • REST, GraphQL, and SOAP API testing against the OWASP API Security Top 10
  • Access control and IDOR testing, including multi-tenant isolation
  • Business logic abuse: pricing, workflow bypass, race conditions
  • Authentication, session, OAuth/OIDC, and SAML implementation review
  • Secure source code review for high-risk components
  • Threat modeling for new features and architectures
  • SAST, DAST, and SCA tooling integration in CI/CD pipelines

Deliverables

  • Executive summary with overall risk posture
  • Detailed findings with CVSS scoring, evidence, and reproduction steps
  • Developer-focused remediation guidance and code examples
  • ASVS coverage matrix
  • Threat model documents (where in scope)
  • Retest report confirming remediation
Outcome: Applications and APIs that withstand real-world abuse, and development teams equipped to keep them that way.

Web Penetration Testing

Adversarial testing of your internet-facing web estate (applications, servers, gateways, and the infrastructure behind them) to demonstrate what an external attacker could actually achieve.

Where web application testing goes deep on a single application, web penetration testing takes the attacker's wide-angle view. We discover your exposed web footprint (including forgotten subdomains, staging sites, and cloud-hosted assets), identify weaknesses across the application, web server, and hosting layers, and chain them together to show real impact: data access, account takeover, or a foothold into internal networks. Testing follows recognized methodologies (OWASP WSTG, PTES) and can be performed black-box, grey-box, or white-box.

What's included

  • Attack surface discovery: subdomains, virtual hosts, exposed admin panels
  • Web server, framework, and CMS vulnerability identification
  • TLS configuration and HTTP security header review
  • WAF and CDN bypass testing, including origin server exposure
  • Injection, deserialization, SSRF, and file upload exploitation
  • Credential attacks against login portals (within agreed limits)
  • Cloud storage and metadata service exposure checks
  • Exploit chaining to demonstrate end-to-end business impact
  • Post-exploitation pivot assessment (where authorized)

Deliverables

  • External web attack surface inventory
  • Attack narratives showing exploit chains and impact
  • Risk-rated findings with evidence and remediation steps
  • Executive summary suitable for boards, customers, and auditors
  • Letter of attestation for third-party assurance requests
  • Remediation verification retest
Outcome: A clear picture of how your web presence could be breached, and evidence to customers and auditors that it has been tested and fixed.

Identity & Access Management

Strategy, design, and implementation of identity governance, authentication, and privileged access so that the right people have the right access, for the right reasons, for the right amount of time.

Effective IAM spans the whole identity lifecycle. We automate joiner, mover, and leaver processes from your HR system of record, define role- and attribute-based access models, run access certifications that reviewers can complete meaningfully, and lock down privileged accounts with just-in-time elevation and session controls. Authentication is strengthened with phishing-resistant MFA such as FIDO2 security keys and passkeys, and everything is aligned to Zero Trust principles. As applications and AI agents increasingly connect to each other directly, we also bring app-to-app access under the control of your identity provider using Cross App Access (XAA) and the Identity Assertion JWT Authorization Grant (ID-JAG), replacing scattered user consents and long-lived tokens with centrally governed, auditable policies. We are platform-neutral and implement across leading IGA, PAM, and access management products.

What's included

  • IAM maturity assessment and Zero Trust identity roadmap
  • Identity lifecycle automation (joiner / mover / leaver) from HR sources
  • Identity governance and administration: SailPoint, Saviynt, Entra ID Governance
  • Role engineering, RBAC/ABAC models, and entitlement clean-up
  • Access certification campaign design and execution
  • Privileged access management: CyberArk, BeyondTrust, Delinea, Entra PIM
  • Phishing-resistant MFA rollout (FIDO2, passkeys, Windows Hello for Business)

Agentic AI Security

Secure AI agents that reason, plan, and act on your behalf, calling tools, APIs, and other agents, before their autonomy becomes an attacker's leverage.

Agentic systems change the threat model. An agent that can read email, query databases, run code, or trigger payments turns a single manipulated input into real-world action. Typical failures include excessive agency, over-privileged tool credentials, poisoned memory, unsafe tool and Model Context Protocol (MCP) server integrations, and multi-agent trust chains nobody designed. We threat-model your agent architectures, design least-privilege identity and authorization for every tool an agent can call, place human approval where impact is high, and red team the result to prove an agent can't be talked into misusing its access.

What's included

  • Agent identity design: per-agent credentials, short-lived tokens, workload identity federation
  • Least-privilege tool scoping, allow-lists, and authorization checks outside the model
  • Human-in-the-loop approval for high-impact actions
  • MCP server and third-party tool supply chain vetting
  • Memory and context poisoning defenses; multi-agent trust boundaries
  • Sandboxing for code execution and browser-using agents
  • Agent observability: action logging, tracing, anomaly detection, and kill switches
